# Stake STX with FP Validated

Stacks secures its blocks with signers, and STX holders back a signer by staking to it. Since the PoX-5 upgrade (Epoch 4.0, July 2026), you stake by naming a signer's **signer-manager contract** in the `stake` call of the `pox-5` contract. Your STX stays in your own address and is locked there for the period you choose. FP Validated never takes custody of it.

PoX-5 replaced the older "stacking" with pools and `delegate-stx`. Positions from PoX-4 were released at the upgrade, so if you used to stack with FP Validated or anyone else, you need to stake again.

:::tip[Fastest way to stake]
Open [Leather's Bring your own signer manager page](https://app.leather.io/staking/pool/byosm), paste FP Validated's signer-manager contract `SP2EC646NK42FKRJA69SYCV6FDQN1HJE4FQK9HZ7B.signer-manager`, connect your wallet and follow the steps. Leather checks that the contract exists and is registered with PoX-5. Check the contract against the table below before you sign.
:::

This page does not quote APY. The yield depends on how much BTC miners commit and how much STX is staked, and it changes every cycle.

## Before you start

- A Stacks wallet. [Leather](https://leather.io) supports PoX-5 staking. Hardware wallets work through Leather.
- The STX you want to stake, plus a little STX left unlocked for transaction fees. You need fees again to claim rewards and to unstake.
- An address with no other staking position. Each address can hold one staking position at a time, so if the address already stakes to another signer, change that position instead (see [Change or add to your position](#change-or-add-to-your-position)).
- For institutions: a custody platform that can call `pox-5` on Stacks.

## Stake

1. Open [Leather Staking](https://app.leather.io/staking) and choose **Bring your own signer manager**, or go straight to the [direct page](https://app.leather.io/staking/pool/byosm).
2. Paste `SP2EC646NK42FKRJA69SYCV6FDQN1HJE4FQK9HZ7B.signer-manager` and select **Continue**.
3. Connect your wallet.
4. Enter the amount of STX and the number of cycles to lock, from 1 to 96. One cycle is about two weeks.
5. If the app offers it, choose whether to receive rewards in sBTC (the default) or in BTC at a Bitcoin address you enter.
6. Review the transaction in your wallet and sign it.

App labels change between versions. If your screen looks different, look for the step that asks for a signer-manager contract.

### What to expect after you stake

- The transaction ID only shows that the transaction was submitted. Open your address on the [Hiro Explorer](https://explorer.hiro.so/?chain=mainnet) to confirm the `stake` call succeeded.
- Your STX locks at the start of the next reward cycle and starts earning from that cycle.
- Staking is rejected during the prepare phase, the last 100 Bitcoin blocks of each cycle (about 17 hours). If your transaction fails with `ERR_STAKE_IN_PREPARE_PHASE`, wait for the next cycle to start and try again.

## Rewards

- Rewards come from the BTC that Stacks miners commit. PoX-5 converts them to sBTC, a 1:1 Bitcoin-backed token on Stacks, and distributes them every 1,050 Bitcoin blocks, about once a week.
- Rewards are not added to your stake. They are held in the signer-manager contract until they are claimed. Claiming is open to anyone, so you can claim your own rewards in Leather. The claim moves your share to your address as sBTC.
- If you set a Bitcoin payout address, the claim starts an sBTC withdrawal and the reward arrives as BTC at that address. A claim smaller than the Bitcoin fee limit you set fails, so let small rewards build up before claiming to BTC.
- If you change your position later and leave the Bitcoin address out, your payout goes back to sBTC.
- Commission is taken from rewards before they are paid out, never from your STX.

## Change or add to your position

A position is changed with the `stake-update` call of `pox-5`, from your wallet's staking screen where it offers one. You can raise the amount, extend the lock or move to another signer-manager. The change takes effect from the next cycle, with no cooldown. Lowering the amount takes one extra cycle.

## Unstake

1. Open your position and choose to unstake. This is not possible during the prepare phase.
2. Sign the transaction.
3. Your STX unlocks at the start of the next reward cycle. If you do nothing, it unlocks when the lock you chose ends.

Rewards already earned stay claimable after you unstake.

## Fees

- **Signer commission:** 5% of rewards, set in the signer-manager contract. The contract admin can change it. The rate that applies to a cycle is fixed when that cycle's rewards are first claimed. You can read the current rate with the contract's `get-fee-bips-for-cycle` function on the explorer.
- **Transaction fees:** staking, claiming and unstaking each cost a small STX fee. A BTC payout also pays the Bitcoin network fee for the withdrawal, up to the limit you set.

## Risks

- **No slashing.** PoX-5 does not slash. If a signer goes offline or misbehaves, it and its stakers lose rewards for that period, not principal.
- **Minimum signer stake.** A signer earns rewards for a cycle only if at least 50,000 STX in total is staked to it. FP Validated is above this threshold.
- **Lock-up.** Your STX stays locked until the next cycle boundary at the earliest, and the price of STX and BTC can move while you wait.
- **Commission risk.** The contract admin can change the commission. Check it before large stakes and when you review your position.
- **Signing risk.** Always check the signer-manager contract before signing. FP Validated will never ask you to send STX to an address.

See [Responsiveness and Slashing Policy](/fp-validated-infra/responsiveness-and-slashing-policy) for how FP Validated responds to validator incidents.

## Institutional staking

Institutions can stake from custody platforms that support PoX-5. Give your custodian the signer-manager contract in the key facts table, and [contact FP Validated](/staking#need-help) to confirm availability on your custodian and to arrange reporting.

## FAQ

**Is there a minimum amount?**
No. PoX-5 has no minimum for each staker. The 50,000 STX threshold applies to the total staked to a signer, and FP Validated already meets it.

**Do I get liquid tokens for my staked STX?**
No. Native PoX-5 staking locks STX in your own address and gives you no receipt token.

**I staked and see no rewards yet. Is something wrong?**
No. Your lock starts at the next cycle, and rewards are distributed about once a week after that. Rewards also stay in the contract until they are claimed.

**What happened to stacking with a pool operator?**
PoX-5 removed pool operators and `delegate-stx`. Every pool is now a signer-manager contract that you name when you stake.

**What happens if FP Validated goes offline?**
Your STX is not at risk. Rewards for the affected period can be lower. Incidents are posted on the [status page](https://fpvalidated.statuspage.io/).
