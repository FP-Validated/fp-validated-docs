# Stake with FP Validated

FP Validated runs validators for proof-of-stake networks and accepts delegation from individuals and institutions. You keep custody of your tokens at all times: staking is a transaction you sign from your own wallet or custody platform, and FP Validated never holds your keys or funds.

- **Commission** is the share of your staking rewards that the validator keeps. It is never taken from the amount you stake.
- **Wait to unstake** is how long your tokens stay locked after you ask for them back. Check it before you stake money you may need at short notice.
- **Rewards** shows whether rewards are added to your stake by the network or whether you claim them with a transaction.

**LayerZero (Zero):** ZRO staking is open, but until the unlock date, expected by 2027-03-31, stakers cannot choose a validator. Stake is split equally across the genesis validators that LayerZero Labs selects, so it is not in the table above. See [Stake ZRO](/staking/layerzero) for how it works.

Each guide opens with a direct link to FP Validated's validator in that network's official staking app, so you do not have to search a validator list.

## Choose how you stake

| Path | Best for | How it works |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Your own wallet | Individuals and small teams | Open the direct link in the chain guide, connect your wallet and sign the staking transaction. |
| Custody platforms | Institutions holding assets at a qualified custodian | Stake from the custodian's console using the validator address or ID in the chain guide. Contact FP Validated to confirm the validator is listed for your custodian. |

## Before you sign

1. **Check the validator.** Compare the address or ID in your wallet with the key facts table in the guide. Validator names can be copied by anyone; addresses cannot.
2. **Keep some tokens unstaked.** Every later step, including claiming rewards and unstaking, costs gas. If you stake your whole balance you cannot pay for it.
3. **Know the wait to unstake.** The table above shows it for each network.
4. **Read what your wallet asks you to sign.** A staking transaction never asks for your recovery phrase and never sends tokens to a person. FP Validated will not message you first to ask for either.

## Risks that apply to every chain

- **Validator performance.** Rewards depend on the validator staying online and following the protocol. Some networks reduce rewards or slash stake when a validator misbehaves. Each guide states the exact rule for that network.
- **Commission changes.** Validators can change their commission. Each guide links to an explorer that shows the live rate.
- **Lock-up and liquidity.** Many networks keep staked tokens locked for a period after you unstake, and the token price can move while you wait.
- **Wallet and signing risk.** Only sign transactions you understand.

FP Validated's incident response targets and slashing accountability model are described in [Responsiveness and Slashing Policy](/fp-validated-infra/responsiveness-and-slashing-policy).

Each guide shows the date its facts were last verified. The comparison table and the key facts table in every guide are generated from `chains/<chain>/staking.yaml`, so the validator address and commission come from one reviewed source. If a value on a guide does not match what your wallet or an explorer shows, trust the chain and tell us so we can correct the page.
