# Stake INJ with FP Validated

Injective uses delegated proof-of-stake built on the Cosmos SDK. You delegate INJ from your own address to a validator, and your tokens never leave your control.

## Before you start

- A wallet that Injective Hub supports, such as [MetaMask](https://metamask.io) or [Keplr](https://www.keplr.app). The Hub lists every supported wallet, including hardware wallets, when you select the connect button.
- INJ in that wallet on Injective Mainnet. INJ held as an ERC-20 token on Ethereum or on an exchange must be bridged or withdrawn to Injective first.
- Enough INJ to stake, plus a little left unstaked for gas. You need gas again to claim rewards and to unstake.

## Stake with Injective Hub

1. Open [FP Validated on Injective Hub](https://injhub.com/validators/injvaloper1kfs4s96fmp00astyd56w4hpls2w2a5tre78nxd/) and connect your wallet. The Hub asks you to accept its terms the first time.
2. Confirm the validator operator address matches the key facts table. Do not rely on the name alone.
3. Choose the stake (delegate) action and enter the amount of INJ.
4. Review and approve the transaction in your wallet.

### What to expect after you stake

- The delegation is active as soon as the transaction confirms. There is no warm-up period.
- Rewards start building up from the next block and show as claimable rewards in Injective Hub.
- Your staked balance does not grow by itself. Rewards stay separate until you claim them.

## Rewards

- Rewards accrue every block but are **not** compounded automatically. Claim them from Injective Hub and stake them again if you want them to compound.
- Commission is taken from rewards, not principal.

## Unstake

1. In Injective Hub, open your delegation to FP Validated and choose the unstake (undelegate) action.
2. Approve the transaction.
3. Your INJ returns to your address automatically when the 21-day unbonding period ends. You do not need to send a second transaction.

During the 21 days the INJ earns no rewards and cannot be transferred or sold, and you cannot shorten the wait. You can have at most 7 unbonding requests from the same validator in progress at once.

### Move stake without waiting

Injective Hub also offers redelegation, which moves stake from one validator to another immediately with no 21-day wait. After you redelegate to a validator, you cannot redelegate that stake onward from it for 21 days.

## Fees

- **Validator commission:** shown in the key facts table. Injective Hub shows the live rate.
- **Gas:** each staking, claiming and unstaking transaction pays a small gas fee in INJ.

## Risks

- **Slashing.** Injective describes slashing of delegated stake for double signing and for downtime. When we read the on-chain parameters on 2026-11-20, both slashing fractions were 0%, but governance can change them, so treat slashing as possible.
- **Jailing.** A validator that signs fewer than 50% of the last 100,000 blocks is jailed, and its delegators earn no rewards while it is jailed.
- **Unbonding period.** Unstaked tokens are locked for 21 days, and the INJ price can move while you wait.
- **Signing risk.** Always check the validator address before signing.

See [Responsiveness and Slashing Policy](/fp-validated-infra/responsiveness-and-slashing-policy) for how FP Validated responds to incidents.

## Institutional staking

Institutions can stake INJ from custody platforms that support Injective delegation. Give your custodian the validator operator address in the key facts table, and [contact FP Validated](/staking#need-help) to confirm availability on your custodian.

## FAQ

**Do I need to claim rewards?**
Yes. Rewards build up until you claim them. Claiming and staking them again is how you compound.

**When do I get my tokens back after unstaking?**
After 21 days. They return to your address automatically.

**Can I switch validators without waiting 21 days?**
Yes, with a redelegation. See [Move stake without waiting](#move-stake-without-waiting).

**What happens if FP Validated goes offline?**
If the validator is jailed you earn no rewards until it is active again. Incidents are posted on the [status page](https://fpvalidated.statuspage.io/).
