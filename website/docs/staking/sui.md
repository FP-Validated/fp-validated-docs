# Stake SUI with FP Validated

Sui uses delegated proof-of-stake. When you stake, your SUI is wrapped in a `StakedSui` object that stays in your own address and records which validator pool it belongs to. FP Validated never takes custody of the SUI you stake.

:::tip Fastest way to stake
Open [FP Validated on SuiVision](https://suivision.xyz/myspace?feature=Stake&validatorAddress=0x13d90417a640de83e0b83683f12028961de06195ab004156df80111a9499081b). The link opens the staking form with FP Validated already selected. Connect your wallet, check the validator address against the table below, enter an amount and sign.
:::

## Before you start

- A Sui wallet that supports staking, such as [Slush](https://slush.app). Other wallets work if they let you pick a validator.
- At least 1 SUI to stake, plus a little SUI left unstaked for gas. You need gas again when you unstake.
- For institutions: a custody platform that can sign Sui transactions.

## Stake

You can stake from the direct link above or from inside your wallet. Both create the same on-chain stake.

### From the direct link

1. Open [FP Validated on SuiVision](https://suivision.xyz/myspace?feature=Stake&validatorAddress=0x13d90417a640de83e0b83683f12028961de06195ab004156df80111a9499081b) and connect your wallet.
2. Confirm the validator address matches the key facts table.
3. Enter the amount of SUI and approve the transaction in your wallet.

### From the Slush wallet

1. Open Slush and go to the **Earn** tab.
2. Choose **Native Staking**. Other products on the Earn tab are not native staking and do not delegate to a validator you pick.
3. Search for **FP Validated** and confirm the validator address matches the key facts table. Do not rely on the name alone.
4. Enter the amount and select **Stake**, then approve the transaction.

Wallet labels change between versions. If your screen looks different, look for the option that lets you choose a validator.

### What to expect after you stake

- The stake appears in your wallet once the transaction confirms, but it is not active yet.
- It becomes active when the next epoch starts, which is at most about 24 hours later.
- The first rewards appear after your stake has been active for one full epoch, so allow up to about 48 hours from the moment you stake.
- You can check the position at any time on the wallet's home screen or by opening your address on [Suiscan](https://suiscan.xyz/mainnet/home).

## Rewards

- Rewards are calculated at the end of every epoch (about 24 hours) and added to the value of your stake. There is nothing to claim and no extra transaction to compound.
- You earn rewards only for epochs in which your stake was active for the whole epoch.
- Commission is taken from rewards, not from your principal. With an 11% commission, FP Validated receives 11% of the rewards your stake earns.

## Unstake

1. Open the staked position in your wallet and choose to unstake.
2. Approve the transaction.
3. Your principal and accrued rewards return to your address as SUI in the same transaction. Sui has no unbonding period.

Two things to know before you unstake:

- **Rewards for the epoch in progress are not paid** when you unstake partway through it. Unstaking shortly after an epoch starts loses the least.
- **Slush unstakes a whole position at a time.** To take out part of your stake, unstake the position and stake the part you want to keep again. If you expect to need partial withdrawals, make several smaller stakes instead of one large one.

## Fees

- **Validator commission:** shown in the key facts table. A commission change on Sui takes effect at the next epoch, and the protocol does not require notice.
- **Gas:** staking and unstaking each cost a small SUI gas fee, paid by your wallet.

## Risks

- **No principal slashing.** Sui does not slash staked principal.
- **Reward withholding (tallying rule).** Validators score each other's performance every epoch. If a validator is reported by enough of the committee, it receives no rewards for that epoch, and neither do its stakers.
- **Commission risk.** A validator can raise its commission for the next epoch. Check the explorer before large stakes and when reviewing positions.
- **Signing risk.** Always check the validator address before signing. FP Validated will never ask you to send SUI to an address.

See [Responsiveness and Slashing Policy](/fp-validated-infra/responsiveness-and-slashing-policy) for how FP Validated responds to validator incidents.

## Institutional staking

Institutions can stake SUI from custody platforms that support Sui delegation. Give your custodian the validator address in the key facts table, and [contact FP Validated](/staking#need-help) to confirm availability on your custodian and to arrange reporting.

## FAQ

**Do I need to claim rewards?**
No. Rewards are added to your stake automatically and are paid out when you unstake.

**I staked an hour ago and see no rewards. Is something wrong?**
No. New stake becomes active at the next epoch and earns its first rewards at the end of that epoch, so the first rewards can take up to about 48 hours to appear.

**Can I split my stake between validators?**
Yes. Each stake is a separate `StakedSui` object, so you can stake to several validators. Each object must hold at least 1 SUI.

**How long does unstaking take?**
Unstaking returns your SUI in the same transaction. There is no waiting period.

**What happens if FP Validated goes offline?**
Your principal is not at risk. If the validator committee reports poor performance for an epoch, that epoch's rewards are withheld. Incidents are posted on the [status page](https://fpvalidated.statuspage.io/).
