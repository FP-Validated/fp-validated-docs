# Stake WAL with FP Validated

Walrus is a storage network that runs on Sui. WAL holders delegate to storage nodes, and each epoch the network assigns storage shards in proportion to stake. Stakers earn a share of the storage fees that their node collects. Your staked WAL stays in your own Sui address as a staked WAL object.

:::tip[Fastest way to stake]
Open the [Walrus staking app](https://stake-wal.wal.app), connect a Sui wallet and select **Stake** next to FP Validated. The app opens on the full node list, so you need to find the node yourself. Check the storage node ID against the table below.
:::

:::caution[Plan around two-week epochs]
Walrus Mainnet epochs last two weeks. New stake can take up to three weeks to start earning, and getting WAL back takes one to four weeks. Read [When stake becomes active](#when-stake-becomes-active) and [Unstake](#unstake) before you stake WAL you may need soon.
:::

## Before you start

- A Sui wallet such as [Slush](https://slush.app) holding at least 1 WAL.
- A small amount of SUI for gas. Walrus staking transactions run on Sui and pay gas in SUI, not WAL. You need SUI again to unstake and to withdraw.

## Stake with the Walrus staking app

1. Open the [Walrus staking app](https://stake-wal.wal.app) and connect your wallet. The node list can take around 20 seconds to load.
2. Look at the epoch progress shown at the top of the page. It tells you how far the current epoch has run and how many days are left, which decides when your stake starts earning (see the next section).
3. Find **FP Validated** under **Current Committee**. The default order is random, so change the sort order or use your browser's find-in-page instead of scrolling. If you cannot find it there, look under **All Storage Nodes**.
4. Confirm the storage node ID matches the key facts table. You can also open the node on [Walruscan](https://walruscan.com/mainnet/operator/0x61d5598a35e198e6cafac7eb808191da3742a2a1789716ab89a68a1f934ee5c6/operator) to compare.
5. Select **Stake**, enter the amount of WAL, and approve the transaction in your wallet.
6. Your position appears under **Your Staked WAL** in the same app. That is also where you unstake and withdraw later.

## When stake becomes active

The committee for each epoch is chosen at the midpoint of the previous epoch, so the date you stake decides when rewards start.

| When you stake | Stake starts earning | Longest wait |
| --- | --- | --- |
| In the first half of an epoch (progress under 50%) | At the start of the next epoch | About 2 weeks |
| In the second half of an epoch (progress over 50%) | One epoch later | About 3 weeks |

If the epoch is just past its midpoint, you lose nothing by staking now, but your WAL will sit idle for most of three weeks. Staking shortly before a midpoint gives the shortest wait.

## Rewards

- Rewards are distributed at the end of every two-week epoch and accrue to your staked WAL.
- There is nothing to claim. You receive rewards together with your principal when you withdraw.
- Commission is taken from rewards, not principal. FP Validated's commission is shown in the key facts table, and Walruscan shows the commission of every node if you want to compare.

## Unstake

Getting WAL back takes two transactions, with a wait between them.

1. In the staking app, find your stake with FP Validated under **Your Staked WAL** and select **Unstake**. The stake is now withdrawing and is assigned a withdraw epoch.
2. When that epoch starts, return to the app and select **Withdraw** to receive your principal and rewards. The WAL does not arrive by itself; you must send this second transaction.

How long you wait depends on when you unstake:

| When you unstake | Stake keeps earning until | WAL can be withdrawn after |
| --- | --- | --- |
| In the first half of an epoch | The end of the current epoch | 1 to 2 weeks |
| In the second half of an epoch | The end of the next epoch | 2 to 3 weeks |

These ranges follow the midpoint rule in the Walrus docs. The Walrus blog tells stakers to allow 14 to 28 days, so plan for up to a month.

Stake that has not become active yet can be withdrawn directly.

## Fees

- **Storage node commission:** shown in the key facts table. Check the explorer for the current and next-epoch rate before staking.
- **Gas:** staking, unstaking and withdrawing each cost a small amount of SUI.

## Risks

- **No principal slashing.** Walrus does not slash delegated WAL. If a node is slashed, the operator's accumulated commission is burned instead.
- **Performance risk.** A node that performs poorly in storage audits earns fewer rewards, which reduces what its stakers receive.
- **Liquidity.** Unstaked WAL takes one to four weeks to become available, and you cannot shorten that wait.

See [Responsiveness and Slashing Policy](/fp-validated-infra/responsiveness-and-slashing-policy) for how FP Validated responds to incidents.

## Institutional staking

Institutions can stake WAL from custody platforms that support Walrus delegation. Give your custodian the storage node ID in the key facts table, and [contact FP Validated](/staking#need-help) to confirm availability on your custodian.

## FAQ

**Do I need to claim rewards?**
No. Rewards accrue to your stake and are paid out when you withdraw.

**I staked days ago and my stake is still not earning. Is something wrong?**
Probably not. Stake added in the second half of an epoch skips the next epoch and starts earning in the one after, which can be about three weeks later.

**I selected Unstake. Where is my WAL?**
Unstaking only starts the wait. Return to the staking app when the withdraw epoch starts and select **Withdraw**.

**Can I move my stake to another node?**
Yes, but there is no direct transfer. Unstake from FP Validated, withdraw, and stake the WAL with the other node. Both steps follow the epoch timing rules.
