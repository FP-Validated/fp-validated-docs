# Stake INIT with FP Validated

Initia uses delegated proof-of-stake built on the Cosmos SDK. You delegate INIT from your own address to a validator, and your tokens never leave your control.

:::tip Fastest way to stake
Open [INIT staking in the Initia App](https://app.initia.xyz/liquidity/uinit/provide). Staking sits under **Liquidity** in the app, not under a menu called Staking. Connect your wallet, enter an amount, and pick **FP Validated** when the app asks for a validator. Check the validator address against the table below.
:::

## Before you start

- A wallet that the Initia App supports. The app lists the options when you connect.
- INIT on Initia Mainnet (the Initia L1, `interwoven-1`). INIT on a rollup or an exchange must be bridged or withdrawn to the L1 first.
- Enough INIT to stake, plus a little left unstaked for gas. You need gas again to claim rewards and to unstake.

Initia also lets you stake whitelisted liquidity-pool tokens and offers lock staking with a fixed lock period. This guide covers plain INIT delegation with no lock.

## Stake with the Initia App

1. Open [INIT staking in the Initia App](https://app.initia.xyz/liquidity/uinit/provide) and connect your wallet. From the app's home page the same screen is under **Liquidity**, then **INIT**. The app asks you to accept its terms the first time.
2. Enter the amount of INIT and continue.
3. When the app asks for a validator, choose **FP Validated** and confirm the validator operator address matches the key facts table. Do not rely on the name alone.
4. If the app asks for a lock duration, choose no lock unless you want lock staking. Locked stake cannot be unstaked until the lock ends.
5. Review and approve the transaction in your wallet.

We wrote these steps from the app's navigation and public walkthroughs, and could not run the flow with a connected wallet. If a screen differs, follow the app and check the validator address.

### What to expect after you stake

- The delegation is active as soon as the transaction confirms. There is no warm-up period.
- Rewards start building up from the next block and show as claimable rewards in the Initia App.
- Your staked balance does not grow by itself. Rewards stay separate until you claim them.

## Rewards

- Rewards accrue every block but are **not** compounded automatically. Claim them from the Initia App and stake them again if you want them to compound.
- Commission is taken from rewards, not principal.

## Unstake

1. In the Initia App, open your staked INIT position with FP Validated and choose the unstake (undelegate) action.
2. Approve the transaction.
3. Your INIT returns to your address automatically when the 21-day unbonding period ends. You do not need to send a second transaction.

During the 21 days the INIT earns no rewards and cannot be transferred or sold, and you cannot shorten the wait. You can have at most 7 unbonding requests from the same validator in progress at once. To move stake to another validator without the wait, use redelegation if the Initia App offers it for your position.

## Fees

- **Validator commission:** shown in the key facts table. Check the explorer for the live rate.
- **Gas:** each staking, claiming and unstaking transaction pays a small gas fee in INIT.

## Risks

- **Double-sign slashing.** If a validator double signs, 5% of the stake delegated to it is slashed, including yours. FP Validated signs with a distributed remote signer to reduce double-sign risk.
- **Jailing.** Downtime slashing is 0%, but a validator that signs fewer than 75% of the last 10,000 blocks is jailed, and its delegators earn no rewards while it is jailed.
- **Unbonding period.** Unstaked tokens are locked for 21 days, and the INIT price can move while you wait.
- **Signing risk.** Always check the validator address before signing.

See [Responsiveness and Slashing Policy](/fp-validated-infra/responsiveness-and-slashing-policy) for how FP Validated responds to incidents.

## Institutional staking

Institutions can stake INIT from custody platforms that support Initia delegation. Give your custodian the validator operator address in the key facts table, and [contact FP Validated](/staking#need-help) to confirm availability on your custodian.

## FAQ

**I cannot find a Staking menu in the Initia App. Where is it?**
Under **Liquidity**. Initia treats INIT staking and liquidity-pool staking as the same feature.

**Do I need to claim rewards?**
Yes. Rewards build up until you claim them. Claiming and staking them again is how you compound.

**When do I get my tokens back after unstaking?**
After 21 days. They return to your address automatically.

**What happens if FP Validated goes offline?**
Downtime does not reduce your principal. If the validator is jailed you earn no rewards until it is active again. Incidents are posted on the [status page](https://fpvalidated.statuspage.io/).
