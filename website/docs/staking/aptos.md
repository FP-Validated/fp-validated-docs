# Stake APT with FP Validated

FP Validated runs an Aptos delegation pool. Anyone can add APT to the pool, and the pool stakes it with FP Validated's validator. Your share of the pool belongs to your own address.

:::tip[Fastest way to stake]
Open the [FP Validated pool on the Aptos Explorer](https://explorer.aptoslabs.com/validator/0x48a7c513805eb2636d420879f26ec36ce9b22f7adcf32ef8f618058f80552696?network=mainnet), connect your wallet and select **Stake**. The explorer lists pools by address, not by name, so use this link instead of searching, and check the pool address against the table below.
:::

## Before you start

- An Aptos wallet such as [Petra](https://petra.app).
- At least 11 APT for your first stake, plus a little left unstaked for gas. You need gas again to unstake and to withdraw.
- An idea of when you may need the APT back. Unstaked APT is released only when the pool's 14-day lockup cycle ends, so the wait can be anything from hours to 14 days.

## Stake

1. Open the [FP Validated pool on the Aptos Explorer](https://explorer.aptoslabs.com/validator/0x48a7c513805eb2636d420879f26ec36ce9b22f7adcf32ef8f618058f80552696?network=mainnet) and connect your wallet.
2. Confirm the delegation pool address matches the key facts table, and that the page shows the commission in the table.
3. Select **Stake**, enter the amount of APT and approve the transaction in your wallet.

If your wallet has its own staking screen, you can stake to the same pool from there. Make sure it lets you choose the pool by address, and use the address in the key facts table.

### What to expect after you stake

- Your stake appears under **My Deposits** on the same explorer page.
- It becomes active at the next epoch. Epochs last about 2 hours.
- The staked amount shown is slightly lower than what you sent. The pool withholds a small add-stake fee so that new stake does not collect rewards for an epoch it was not active in.

## Rewards

- Rewards are added to your stake every epoch and compound automatically. There is nothing to claim.
- Rewards depend on how many of its block proposals the validator completes successfully.
- Commission is taken from rewards, not principal.

## Unstake

Getting APT back takes two transactions, with a wait between them.

1. On the explorer page, find your stake under **My Deposits** and select **Unstake**. Enter the amount and approve the transaction.
2. The unstaked APT stays pending until the pool's current 14-day lockup cycle ends. The explorer shows the time left until the next unlock. Pending APT keeps earning rewards until then.
3. After the lockup ends, select **Withdraw** to move the APT to your address. It does not arrive by itself.

Rules for partial unstaking:

- Unstake at least 10 APT at a time.
- Leave at least 10 APT staked, or unstake everything. If a partial unstake would leave less than 10 APT, the pool unstakes the whole position.

The delegation pool contract also allows pending stake to be reactivated before the lockup ends. Whether you can do that from a wallet or the explorer depends on the app.

## Fees

- **Operator commission:** shown in the key facts table. A commission change takes effect from the next lockup cycle.
- **Add-stake fee:** described under [What to expect after you stake](#what-to-expect-after-you-stake). It is a protocol rule, not a fee paid to FP Validated.
- **Gas:** each transaction costs a small amount of APT.

## Risks

- **No slashing.** The Aptos framework does not slash stake.
- **Performance risk.** Missed or failed block proposals reduce rewards for the whole pool.
- **Lockup.** Unstaked APT is not available until the end of the current 14-day lockup cycle, and the APT price can move while you wait.
- **Signing risk.** Always check the pool address before signing.

See [Responsiveness and Slashing Policy](/fp-validated-infra/responsiveness-and-slashing-policy) for how FP Validated responds to incidents.

## Institutional staking

Institutions can stake APT from custody platforms that support Aptos delegation pools. Give your custodian the delegation pool address in the key facts table, and [contact FP Validated](/staking#need-help) to confirm availability on your custodian.

## FAQ

**Do I need to claim rewards?**
No. Rewards compound into your stake every epoch.

**Why 11 APT when the contract minimum is 10?**
The delegation pool requires every delegator to keep at least 10 APT staked. The add-stake fee is taken from your first deposit, so a deposit of exactly 10 APT can end up below the minimum. Staking 11 APT or more avoids that.

**How long does unstaking take?**
Until the end of the pool's current 14-day lockup cycle, so anywhere from a few hours to 14 days. The explorer shows the time left. Then you send a withdraw transaction.

**I unstaked and the lockup has ended, but the APT is not in my wallet.**
Select **Withdraw** on the explorer page. Unstaked APT stays in the pool until you withdraw it.
